# DevSphere Learning Resources

A free, curated collection of learning material for developers at every stage. Every collection points to official documentation, recognized open-source projects, or free educational resources.

> **Principles:** free to access, actively maintained, beginner-friendly, and useful for real practice. DevSphere does not accept paid placements or affiliate links.

## How to use this collection

1. **Start with the official tutorial for your language or tool.** It is usually the most accurate and up-to-date source.
2. **Build something small after each concept.** Reading without practice rarely sticks.
3. **Use documentation as a reference, not a novel.** Learn to search it efficiently.
4. **Follow one path at a time.** Collecting resources is not the same as learning.
5. **Share what you learn.** Write a short note, answer a question, or present a small demo.

## Selection criteria

A resource is included only when it meets most of these criteria:

- **Free to access** without a mandatory subscription.
- **Official or widely recognized** by the technology's maintainers or community.
- **Maintained** with recent updates or stable archival value.
- **Accurate** and consistent with current documentation.
- **Accessible** to a global audience, with clear language and examples.
- **No affiliate or referral links.**

Some resources offer optional paid certificates or hosting. The free learning path must remain usable without payment; optional upgrades are not required by DevSphere.

## Verification

Links are reviewed when the collection is updated and at least quarterly. If a link is broken, outdated, or no longer free, please open a **documentation issue** or a pull request.

## Suggest a resource

Before suggesting a resource:

1. Confirm it is free and actively maintained.
2. Check that it is not already listed.
3. Prefer official documentation over content farms.
4. Explain who it helps and why it belongs in the collection.
5. Disclose any affiliation with the resource.

Open a [feature request](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/issues/new?template=feature_request.md) or a pull request following [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Important note

DevSphere does not control third-party sites. Content, availability, and pricing can change. Verify important information against the official documentation for your version and platform. Never share credentials or private data with an external learning platform.
