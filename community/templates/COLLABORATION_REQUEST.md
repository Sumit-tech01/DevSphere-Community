# Collaboration Request Template

Use this template in [Collaboration](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/collaboration) when you have an existing project and need help with a specific problem.

> Replace every bracketed prompt. Do not post confidential code, customer data, or credentials. State whether the work is paid, unpaid, voluntary, or open source.

## Title

`[Project name] — help with [specific task] ([paid / unpaid / open source])`

## Project

- **What it does:** [one or two sentences]
- **Repository:** [public link or "private; details available after agreement"]
- **Demo:** [link or "no public demo"]
- **License:** [license]
- **Stage:** [prototype, MVP, or production]
- **Maintainer:** [name or team]

## The problem

[What is not working, missing, or too slow? Include error messages, screenshots, or reproduction steps when safe.]

## What I have tried

1. [Attempt and result]
2. [Attempt and result]
3. [What I think the next step is]

This section helps people avoid repeating your work and shows that you have made a good-faith effort.

## The smallest useful contribution

[One task that would unblock the project. For example: "review this query", "add a regression test for this bug", "fix the mobile layout on the settings page", or "write setup instructions for Windows".]

## Skills needed

- **Required:** [skills]
- **Helpful:** [skills]
- **Not required:** [for example, "deep knowledge of the whole codebase"]

## Setup and verification

```text
[commands to install, run, and test]
```

**Known environment issues:** [versions, platform, or "none known"]

**How I will verify the change:** [tests, manual steps, or review]

## Collaboration model

- [ ] **Paid:** [scope, rate or budget, currency, payment schedule]
- [ ] **Unpaid / voluntary:** [no payment; time expectation]
- [ ] **Open source:** [license and contribution process]
- [ ] **Equity or revenue share:** [written terms]

**Time expectation:** [hours or "no fixed expectation"]

## Review and communication

- **Where to discuss:** [this thread or repository issue]
- **Review owner:** [person]
- **Expected review window:** [best-effort estimate]
- **Decision process:** [who merges or approves]
- **Credit:** [how contributors are recognized]

## Boundaries

- Do not share API keys, passwords, private keys, or customer data.
- Do not ask for unpaid work outside the stated scope.
- Do not publish private repository content without permission.
- Do not promise payment or ownership that is not in writing.

## How to help

Reply with:

1. The part you can help with.
2. One relevant example of your work.
3. Your availability.
4. Any setup blocker you see.
