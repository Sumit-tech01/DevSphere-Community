# Find a Teammate Template

Use this template in [Collaboration](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/collaboration) or [Hackathons & Challenges](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/hackathons-challenges) when you need a specific role.

> Replace every bracketed prompt. State the payment or collaboration model near the top. Do not ask for free work disguised as an opportunity.

## Title

`[Project or event] — looking for [role] ([paid / unpaid / open source / team])`

## Project or event

- **Name:** [name]
- **One-line description:** [what it is]
- **Stage:** [idea, prototype, MVP, or production]
- **Links:** [repository, demo, event page, or "not public yet"]
- **Duration:** [start and end, or "ongoing with review"]

## Role I need

**Role:** [for example, frontend developer, designer, data analyst, tester, technical writer]

**What you will work on:**

- [specific task]
- [specific task]
- [specific task]

**Required skills:** [skills]

**Nice-to-have skills:** [skills]

**Not required:** [for example, "no prior open-source experience"]

## Commitment and schedule

- **Time:** [hours per week]
- **Time zone or overlap:** [if relevant]
- **Duration:** [weeks or milestones]
- **Flexibility:** [how deadlines can change]
- **Meeting format:** [async, weekly call, or both]

Be honest about uncertainty. "I am not sure yet" is better than a commitment you cannot keep.

## Collaboration model

Choose one and make it explicit:

- [ ] **Paid:** [rate or budget, currency, payment schedule, contractor or employment relationship]
- [ ] **Unpaid / voluntary:** [state clearly that there is no payment]
- [ ] **Open source:** [license and contribution process]
- [ ] **Equity or revenue share:** [written terms and decision rights]
- [ ] **Hackathon team:** [event rules and submission ownership]

**Expenses:** [reimbursed, not reimbursed, or not applicable]

## How we will work

- **Issue tracking:** [where tasks live]
- **Communication:** [public thread, repository, or chat after agreement]
- **Reviews:** [who reviews and expected turnaround]
- **Decisions:** [who has the final call]
- **Credit:** [how contributors are named]
- **Exit:** [how someone can leave without pressure]

## What I bring

[Your relevant experience, time, design, code, testing, documentation, or project management. Do not overstate it.]

## What I am asking for

[One specific request: a reply with a relevant project, a short call, a code review, or a trial task. Keep the first step small.]

## Safety and privacy

- Do not post identity documents, bank details, or private contact information.
- I will not ask for payment, credentials, or sensitive data to apply.
- I will move to a private channel only after both sides agree.
- I will follow the [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] The role and tasks are specific.
- [ ] The time commitment is realistic.
- [ ] The paid, unpaid, voluntary, or open-source model is explicit.
- [ ] Ownership and credit are addressed.
- [ ] No private data is included.
- [ ] The first step is low-pressure.
