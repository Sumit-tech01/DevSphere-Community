> Replace every bracketed prompt. Remove anything that does not apply. Do not claim users, funding, revenue, or partnerships you cannot verify.

## How to express interest

Reply with:

1. The role or part you want to help with.
2. One relevant project or skill.
3. Your realistic availability.
4. Any question that would help you decide.

Do not post private contact details. Move to a private channel only after both sides agree.

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] The problem and intended user are clear.
- [ ] The first milestone is small and reviewable.
- [ ] The collaboration model is labeled paid, unpaid, voluntary, or open source.
- [ ] Ownership and licensing are stated or explicitly open.
- [ ] Claims are verifiable.
- [ ] No confidential data or credentials are included.
- [ ] The post uses one category.
