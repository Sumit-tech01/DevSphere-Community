# DevSphere Developer Spotlight Interview Template

This template is completed by a developer who has accepted a Spotlight invitation. It is an **asynchronous written interview**, not a test. Answer in your own words, skip anything you do not want to share, and review the final draft before publication.

> **Privacy first:** Do not include a home address, private email, phone number, workplace location, identity document, customer data, or private messages. Use the public links you want published. If a question feels too personal, write "prefer not to answer."
>
> **Permission is required.** A profile is not published until the permission section at the end is completed and the final draft is approved.

## Before you begin

- Read the [Developer Spotlight handbook](../DEVELOPER_SPOTLIGHT.md).
- Decide which name you want published. It does not have to be your legal name.
- Check that every link works in a private browser window.
- Remove credentials, private data, and unverified metrics from screenshots or demos.
- Allow 30–45 minutes. You can return to the template later.
