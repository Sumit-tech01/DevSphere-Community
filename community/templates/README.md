# DevSphere Templates

Reusable formats for proposing projects, finding collaborators, requesting focused project reviews, sharing work, submitting resources, nominating a spotlight, and hosting community activities.

## How to use a template

1. **Choose the closest template.** Use one template per discussion post.
2. **Copy it into a new discussion** in the category named in the template.
3. **Replace every bracketed prompt** with your own context.
4. **Remove sections that do not apply**, but keep the payment or collaboration model.
5. **Check for private data** before posting: no passwords, tokens, customer data, identity documents, or home addresses.
6. **Label opportunities clearly:** paid, unpaid, voluntary, open source, equity, or revenue share.
7. **Do not invent** users, revenue, funding, partnerships, achievements, or deadlines.

## Ground rules

- **Be specific.** A clear ask gets a better response than "any feedback?"
- **Respect time.** State the commitment and do not hide unpaid work.
- **Credit fairly.** Agree on ownership, license, and recognition before work starts.
- **Stay public until trust is established.** Do not ask for sensitive documents in a public thread.
- **No spam.** One category per post; no mass mentions or duplicate posts.
- **No payment for inclusion.** DevSphere does not sell resource listings or spotlight placements.
