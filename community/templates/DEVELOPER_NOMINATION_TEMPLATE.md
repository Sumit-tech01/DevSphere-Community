# DevSphere Developer Nomination Template

Use this template to nominate yourself or another developer for the [DevSphere Developer Spotlight](../DEVELOPER_SPOTLIGHT.md). Post completed nominations in **[General Discussion](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/general-discussion)**.

> **Consent comes first.** You may nominate another developer only with their permission. Do not post someone's name, project, personal story, or contact details without asking them. If you want to suggest someone privately, contact a maintainer through the route in the [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) instead of posting a public nomination.
>
> **A nomination is not selection.** It starts a review. DevSphere does not guarantee a profile, award, prize, certificate, job, internship, funding, or financial benefit. A category may have no selection.

## 5. Why this person deserves recognition

Write 2–4 short paragraphs. Be specific.

- What did they build, fix, document, test, design, explain, or improve?
- Who benefited, and how?
- What evidence can a reviewer verify?
- What did they learn or overcome?
- How did they credit or help others?

Avoid generic praise such as "great developer" or "amazing person." A specific behavior is more useful than a compliment.
