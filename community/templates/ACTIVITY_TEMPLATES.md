# Activity Templates

Copy these formats into the correct DevSphere category. Replace bracketed prompts and remove anything that does not apply.

> Activities run only when a host is available. These templates are not a promise of a weekly or monthly event. Keep posts accessible, specific, and free of invented metrics.

## Monday — Weekly learning goals

**Title:** `Weekly learning goals — [date range]`

**Category:** [General Discussion](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/general-discussion)

**Body:**

- What is one skill you are working on this week?
- What is the smallest deliverable you can finish?
- What is one blocker you expect?
- What resource has helped you so far?
- Optional: one thing you can help someone else with.

> **Host notes:** Reply to each participant with one specific suggestion or connection. Do not turn this into a streak or a public leaderboard. Close with a short summary of shared themes.

## Tuesday — Technical deep dive

**Title:** `Deep dive — [question or concept]`

**Category:** [Programming](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/programming)

**Body:**

- Context: [why this question matters]
- What I have tried: [attempts, docs, or experiments]
- The trade-off I see: [option A versus option B]
- My current understanding: [short explanation]
- What I want to learn: [specific question]

> **Host notes:** Ask for reasoning and evidence, not only opinions. Invite a counterexample. Summarize the strongest answer after the discussion settles. Do not present a benchmark as universal truth.

## Wednesday — AI & ML discussion

**Title:** `AI discussion — [paper, tool, or evaluation question]`

**Category:** [AI & Machine Learning](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/ai-machine-learning)

**Body:**

- What is being discussed?
- What was measured, and on what data?
- What was not measured?
- What are the limitations?
- What would responsible use require?
- What question do you have for the community?

> **Host notes:** Separate demo quality from general capability. Ask about data provenance, evaluation, cost, privacy, and failure modes. Do not promote a product without disclosure.

## Thursday — Coding challenge

**Title:** `Challenge — [challenge name] ([level])`

**Category:** [Hackathons & Challenges](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/hackathons-challenges)

**Body:**

- Challenge: [link to the brief]
- Skills practiced: [list]
- Suggested level: [beginner, intermediate, advanced]
- Suggested time range: [range, not a pressure deadline]
- Definition of done: [specific criteria]
- Where to submit: [Project Showcase with the required title]
- Feedback request: [what kind of review you want]

> **Host notes:** Do not post full solutions without a spoiler warning. Remind participants that a finished, explained attempt is better than an unused perfect plan. Review the rubric before publishing.

## Friday — Open-source Friday

**Title:** `Open-source Friday — [issue, pull request, or lesson]`

**Category:** [Collaboration](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/collaboration)

**Body:**

- Project and link:
- Issue or contribution:
- What I tried:
- What I learned about the project's process:
- Where I need help:
- One maintainer decision worth understanding:

> **Host notes:** Keep the thread practical. Do not pressure maintainers to merge. Remind participants to read contribution guides and avoid low-quality pull requests for profile activity.

## Saturday — Project showcase

**Title:** `Showcase — [project name] ([stage])`

**Category:** [Project Showcase](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/project-showcase)

**Body:** use the [project showcase template](PROJECT_SHOWCASE.md). At minimum:

- What it does and who it helps.
- Demo and repository links.
- One difficult trade-off.
- One specific feedback question.
- Known limitations.

> **Host notes:** Ask each presenter one specific question. Do not let the thread become a link dump. Recognize honest limitations and thoughtful documentation.

## Sunday — Community feedback

**Title:** `Community feedback — [week or topic]`

**Category:** [General Discussion](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/general-discussion)

**Body:**

- What helped you this week?
- What was confusing or hard to find?
- Which category or template needs work?
- What should we stop doing?
- Who should we thank?
- One small change you recommend.

> **Host notes:** Record actionable feedback and follow up with what changed. Do not ask for feedback you cannot act on. Thank people specifically.

## Monthly — Build sprint

**Title:** `Build sprint — [month] — [theme]`

**Category:** [Collaboration](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/collaboration)

**Body:**

- Problem and intended user:
- Smallest deliverable:
- Start and end dates:
- Public progress updates:
- Collaborators welcome: [yes/no and terms]
- Final share: [showcase or write-up]
- Prize: none unless a written sponsor agreement exists

> **Host notes:** A sprint is self-directed. Do not require daily updates or public code. Protect participants from scope creep. Do not invent prizes, sponsors, or winners.

## Monthly — Developer spotlight

**Title:** `Spotlight nomination — [category] — [display name]`

**Category:** [General Discussion](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/general-discussion)

**Body:** use the [spotlight nomination template](SPOTLIGHT_NOMINATION.md). Confirm consent before publishing evidence or personal details.

> **Host notes:** Review evidence, not popularity. Do not publish a spotlight without explicit permission. A category may have no selection. Follow the [Developer Spotlight framework](../DEVELOPER_SPOTLIGHT.md).

## Monthly — Open-source contribution month

**Title:** `Open-source contribution month — share what you learned`

**Category:** [Collaboration](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/collaboration)

**Body:**

- Project and link:
- Contribution type: [docs, test, bug fix, review, translation, design]
- What was reviewed or merged:
- What you learned about the project:
- What you would do differently:
- One resource that helped:

> **Host notes:** Do not rank by commit count or lines changed. A documentation fix can be more valuable than a large unreviewed change. Confirm the contribution is public before linking it.

**Body:**

- Prerequisites:
- Materials or repository:
