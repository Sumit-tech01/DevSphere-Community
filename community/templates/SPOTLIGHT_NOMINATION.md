# Developer Spotlight Nomination Template (Quick Form)

This is the short nomination form. For the full process, criteria, and consent fields, use the [Developer nomination template](DEVELOPER_NOMINATION_TEMPLATE.md) and read the [Developer Spotlight framework](../DEVELOPER_SPOTLIGHT.md) first.

Use this template in [General Discussion](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/general-discussion).

> Nominate with consent. Do not include private contact information, identity documents, or personal details the nominee has not agreed to share. A nomination is not a guarantee of selection.
