# Project Showcase Template

Use this template in [Project Showcase](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/project-showcase) to share what you built and ask for useful feedback.

> Replace every bracketed prompt. Do not claim users, revenue, downloads, awards, or impact you cannot verify. Ask permission before sharing someone else's work, code, or data.

## Feedback etiquette

- Ask before giving unrequested code-level feedback.
- Focus on the requested area.
- Be specific and kind.
- Do not promote your own project in someone else's showcase thread.
- Do not use the thread to recruit unpaid labor without labeling it.
