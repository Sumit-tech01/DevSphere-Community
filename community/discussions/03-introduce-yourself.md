# Introduce Yourself to DevSphere

Every community starts with people, not repositories.

Tell us who you are, what you are learning, and what you hope to build. You do not need an impressive title, a long list of achievements, or years of experience. "I am just starting" is a perfectly good introduction.

## A simple template

Copy this into a reply and fill in what you are comfortable sharing:

```markdown
**Name or nickname:**

**Where I am based (optional):**

**What I do:** Student / Developer / Designer / Freelancer / Founder / Other

**Languages and tools I use:**

**What I am learning right now:**

**What I am building or want to build:**

**What I would like to find in DevSphere:**

**One thing I can help others with:**

**A fun fact (optional):**
```

Share only what you want to share. Your exact location, employer, age, and contact details are not required.

## A few ideas to get you started

- **If you are a student:** What are you studying, and what part of it excites you most?
- **If you are switching careers:** What brought you into technology?
- **If you are a maintainer:** What project are you working on, and where could others help?
- **If you are exploring AI:** What are you learning, and what confuses you?
- **If you are a founder:** What problem are you trying to solve, and what stage are you at?
- **If you are here to help:** What kinds of questions do you enjoy answering?

## How to make a good introduction

1. **Be specific.** "I am learning React and struggling with state management" gives people a way to connect.
2. **Be honest.** You do not need to inflate your experience.
3. **Be kind.** Welcome the person above you if you can.
4. **Be patient.** Replies may take a little time across time zones.
5. **Do not promote.** This is a place to meet people, not to drop a sales pitch.

## What happens next

- Browse the categories that match your interests.
- Reply to someone with a similar background or goal.
- Ask a question in **Questions & Help**.
- Share your work in **Project Showcase** when you are ready.
- Read the [Community Guidelines](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md).

## A small ask

When you reply to someone's introduction, ask one genuine follow-up question. That is how a list of names becomes a community.

Welcome to DevSphere. We are glad you are here.
