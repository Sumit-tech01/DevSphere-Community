# Weekly Community Feedback — Help Shape DevSphere

DevSphere is built in public. That means the community should have a real way to influence what comes next.

This is the weekly feedback thread. It is a place to share what is working, what is confusing, and what you would change.

## What we want to hear

- **Navigation:** Can you find the right category? Are the names clear?
- **Content:** Which resources, guides, or discussions are useful? Which are missing?
- **Programs:** Are the challenges, showcase, and collaboration threads valuable?
- **Moderation:** Is the community living up to its guidelines? (Do not include sensitive details here.)
- **Accessibility:** Is anything hard to read, navigate, or use?
- **Community health:** What would make you participate more? What makes you hesitate?
- **Ideas:** What should DevSphere try, stop, or improve?

## Feedback template

```markdown
**What I tried:**

**What worked well:**

**What was confusing or frustrating:**

**One concrete suggestion:**

**Would I be willing to help with this?** Yes / No / Maybe
```

You do not need to use the template. A short, honest observation is welcome.

## How feedback is used

- Maintainers read every good-faith suggestion.
- Not every suggestion can be implemented; capacity, scope, and community safety matter.
- Decisions may be explained in a follow-up comment or an announcement.
- Significant changes are recorded in the [changelog](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
- Repeated themes help shape the [community roadmap](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/COMMUNITY_ROADMAP.md).

Feedback is not a vote. It is input. The goal is to make better decisions, not to satisfy every request.

## Keep it constructive

- Critique the process, not the person.
- Be specific: describe the behavior or gap you observed.
- Suggest an alternative when you can.
- Assume maintainers are acting in good faith.
- Do not use this thread to relitigate a moderation decision or expose private information.

## Sensitive concerns

If you need to report harassment, a security vulnerability, or a private safety issue, **do not post it here**. Use the private routes in the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [Security Policy](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## A weekly habit

If you have five minutes, answer one question:

**What is one thing DevSphere should keep doing, and one thing it should improve?**

Small observations, shared consistently, are how a community gets better.

Thank you for helping build DevSphere.
