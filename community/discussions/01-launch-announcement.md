# DevSphere Is Live — A Global Home for Developers Who Build Together

**Connect. Code. Collaborate. Create.**

DevSphere is now open.

DevSphere is a free, global developer community hosted entirely on GitHub. It is a place for developers, students, software engineers, AI and machine learning enthusiasts, web and app builders, open-source contributors, freelancers, founders, and technology enthusiasts to learn from one another and build meaningful work.

There is no membership fee, no application form, and no separate platform. A free GitHub account is all you need.

## Why DevSphere exists

Technology grows stronger when knowledge is shared. Yet many developers learn alone, struggle to find collaborators, or hesitate to ask questions because they fear judgment.

DevSphere is built to change that.

We want a community where:

- A beginner can ask a question without being mocked.
- An experienced engineer can share hard-won lessons.
- A builder can find the teammate they need.
- A student can discover a path into open source.
- Someone exploring AI can separate evidence from hype.
- People across time zones can participate without attending a live event.

## What is available today

The DevSphere repository now includes:

- **Community policies** that explain how we treat each other and how moderation works.
- **A discussion guide** for asking better questions and sharing better projects.
- **Curated learning resources** for programming, web development, AI and machine learning, and open source.
- **Challenge 001** — build a responsive developer profile card.
- **Issue and pull request templates** for contributing to the community.
- **A public roadmap** for the next six months.

Everything is free and open to inspection.

## How to get started

1. **Star and watch the repository** so you can follow announcements.
2. **Introduce yourself** in the Introductions category.
3. **Explore the categories** and follow the ones that match your interests.
4. **Ask a question** in Questions & Help, or answer someone else's.
5. **Share what you are building** in Project Showcase.
6. **Try Challenge 001** if you want a practical place to start.

You do not need to be an expert to belong here. Curiosity is enough.

## What we are building next

The first six months focus on a simple, sustainable rhythm: welcome people well, answer questions thoughtfully, run practical challenges, share legitimate opportunities, and improve the community based on feedback.

The full plan is in the [community roadmap](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/COMMUNITY_ROADMAP.md).

## A community is made of people, not infrastructure

This repository is the foundation, but you are the community. The conversations you start, the questions you answer, the projects you share, and the way you treat people will determine what DevSphere becomes.

Be kind. Be specific. Credit others. Ask for help when you need it. Offer help when you can.

**Welcome to DevSphere. Let's build something worth sharing.**
