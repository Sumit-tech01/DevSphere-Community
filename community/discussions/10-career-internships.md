# Career & Internship Opportunities — Share, Ask, and Grow

Career growth is not only about landing a job. It is about understanding your options, building useful skills, learning how hiring works, and finding environments where you can do your best work.

This category is for legitimate opportunities and honest career conversations.

## Share an opportunity

Use this format:

```markdown
## Role

## Organization

## Location / remote policy

## Employment type

Full-time / Part-time / Internship / Contract / Fellowship

## Eligibility

Students, early-career, experienced, geographic restrictions, work authorization.

## Compensation

Range if publicly available. If not disclosed, say so.

## Application link

Official application page only.

## Deadline

## About the role

A short, accurate summary.

## Your connection

Are you the employer, a recruiter, an employee, or sharing on behalf of someone else?
```

## Non-negotiables

- **No pay-to-apply.** Legitimate employers do not ask candidates to pay a fee for a job, interview, training, or equipment.
- **No sensitive documents up front.** Do not ask for bank details, identity documents, or passwords in a public discussion.
- **No pyramid schemes or commission-only roles disguised as jobs.** Label compensation structure honestly.
- **No fake openings.** If a role closes, update or remove the post.
- **No resume harvesting.** Do not collect CVs for an unnamed employer.
- **No discrimination.** Posts must comply with applicable employment law and the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## For job seekers

You can ask for:

- Feedback on a portfolio or resume structure (remove private details).
- Advice on preparing for a specific type of interview.
- How to evaluate an offer or internship.
- How to negotiate scope, mentorship, or learning opportunities.
- How to identify a scam.

Do not post your full resume with your phone number, home address, or personal email. Share a portfolio or a redacted version instead.

## For employers and recruiters

- Be transparent about the organization, role, location, and compensation.
- Respect the community's time; do not mass-message members.
- Answer questions in the thread so everyone can benefit.
- Do not use the community as a free sourcing channel for roles you cannot describe.
- Disclose your affiliation.

## Career discussion starters

- What did you learn in your first three months as a developer?
- How do you evaluate a junior role for mentorship and growth?
- What is one interview question you found genuinely useful?
- How do you decide between a startup and a larger company?
- What advice would you give someone switching into tech?
- How do you keep learning while working full-time?

## A note on advice

Career advice here comes from community members, not licensed career counselors. It is based on personal experience and may not fit your country, industry, or circumstances. Verify legal, immigration, and compensation questions with qualified professionals.

## Disclaimer

DevSphere shares opportunities for community awareness. Posting does not imply endorsement. Always verify the employer, role, pay, and application process through official channels before sharing personal information.
