# 🌟 Nominate a Developer for the DevSphere Spotlight

Do you know someone whose project, contribution, or help deserves a specific thank-you? The DevSphere Developer Spotlight is open for nominations.

This thread is the nomination hub. You can nominate **yourself** or **another developer with their permission**.

## Who can be nominated?

Anyone who participates in DevSphere or has contributed publicly in a way the community can review:

- Beginners and students.
- Self-taught developers and career changers.
- Independent builders and solo founders.
- Open-source contributors and maintainers.
- Designers, technical writers, testers, and accessibility advocates.
- AI and machine-learning practitioners.
- Community helpers who answer questions, review work, or welcome new members.

You do **not** need a large following, a senior title, a degree, a paid product, or a famous repository. A small, well-documented project or a pattern of patient help can be strong evidence.

## What makes a strong nomination?

Specific, verifiable evidence:

- What did the person build, fix, document, test, design, explain, or improve?
- Who benefited, and how?
- What can a reviewer open and check?
- What did the person learn or overcome?
- How did they credit or help others?

A nomination that says "great developer, check out their GitHub" is hard to review. A nomination that says "they added accessible keyboard navigation to this demo, documented the change, and answered three follow-up questions" is useful.

## How to nominate

1. **Read the handbook.** [Developer Spotlight](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/DEVELOPER_SPOTLIGHT.md) explains the criteria, review, and privacy rules.
2. **Ask for permission** if you are nominating another person. Confirm the name, links, and details they are comfortable making public.
3. **Copy the template.** [Developer nomination template](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/templates/DEVELOPER_NOMINATION_TEMPLATE.md)
4. **Post a new discussion** in General Discussion with the title `Developer Spotlight nomination: <name or project>`.
5. **Complete the consent section.** A nomination without clear consent cannot proceed.
6. **Disclose your relationship** to the nominee and any conflict of interest.

Self-nominations are welcome. Mark the self-nomination checkbox so reviewers understand the context.

## What happens next?

1. A maintainer checks completeness, category, and consent.
2. Reviewers check the evidence against the published criteria.
3. Anyone with a conflict of interest does not decide that nomination.
4. Shortlisted nominees receive a private invitation.
5. If they accept, they complete the interview template and approve the final draft.
6. Approved profiles are published in **Announcements**.

**A nomination is not selection.** A category may have no selection in a given week or month. Reviewers may decline a nomination because the evidence is incomplete, the scope is unclear, or there is not enough capacity to do it justice. A decline is not a permanent judgment; new evidence can support a future nomination.

## Consent and privacy

- Do not post another person's name, project, or story without asking them.
- Do not include private contact information, identity documents, customer data, or private messages.
- If you want to suggest someone without their knowledge, contact a maintainer privately instead of posting a public nomination.
- If the nominee is a minor, do not publish personal details without a parent or guardian's informed consent.
- A featured person can request correction or removal at any time.

## What this program is not

It is not a ranking, a prize, a certificate, a job offer, a funding offer, or an endorsement of every claim in a project. DevSphere does not promise money, employment, or financial benefit. It is a community thank-you, built on evidence and consent.

## Questions?

Ask in this thread or in [General Discussion](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/general-discussion). Do not post private information.

> **Nominate someone whose work made a difference. Then let them decide whether to be featured.**
