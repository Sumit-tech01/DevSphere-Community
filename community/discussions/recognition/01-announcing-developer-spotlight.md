# 🌟 Introducing the DevSphere Developer Spotlight

DevSphere is built by people who learn in public, share what they build, and help each other get better. Today we are opening a program that makes that work easier to see: the **DevSphere Developer Spotlight**.

## Why a Spotlight?

Most community work is invisible. A documentation fix, a careful review, a first working prototype, or a patient answer to a beginner rarely shows up in a follower count. The Spotlight exists to notice those contributions and give them a durable, specific thank-you.

It is **not** a ranking, a popularity contest, a certification, or a prize. It is a consented profile of a person and a project they choose to share, based on evidence the community can verify.

**No one is featured yet.** DevSphere will not invent members, projects, achievements, testimonials, or winners. The first spotlight will be published only after a nomination, a review, and explicit permission.

## Four ways to participate

| Initiative | Rhythm | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Developer Spotlight** | Weekly slot | Features a developer and a project with consent |
| **Project Review Friday** | Weekly | Gives constructive, specific feedback on community projects |
| **Builder of the Month** | Monthly | Recognizes meaningful contribution, learning progress, and project development |
| **Community Collaboration** | Ongoing | Helps members find collaborators and build together |

Read the full [Community Initiatives guide](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/COMMUNITY_INITIATIVES.md) for schedules, formats, and moderation rules.

## How the Spotlight works

1. **Nominate.** Nominate yourself or another developer with their permission in [General Discussion](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/general-discussion).
2. **Review.** A small review group checks the evidence against published criteria. Stars, followers, and commit counts are not selection criteria.
3. **Invite.** Shortlisted developers receive a private invitation. They can accept, ask questions, or decline without pressure.
4. **Interview.** The developer completes an asynchronous written interview and can skip any optional question.
5. **Approve.** The developer reviews the final draft for factual accuracy.
6. **Publish.** Approved profiles appear in **Announcements** and are indexed in the Spotlight handbook.

A nomination does not guarantee selection. A week with no ready profile is a normal week; we will not publish a profile just to fill a slot.

## What we will not do

- Publish a profile without explicit permission.
- Rank developers by stars, followers, or popularity.
- Promise money, prizes, certificates, jobs, internships, funding, or investment.
- Sell a spotlight placement or accept affiliate links.
- Publish private contact information or unverified claims.
- Pressure anyone to accept, answer, or stay featured.
