# 🤝 Find Your Next Project Teammate

A good project can stall because the builder is missing one person: a designer, a tester, a writer, a second maintainer, or someone who has solved the problem before.

This is an ongoing thread for finding collaborators in DevSphere. It is not a job board, a recruiting funnel, or a guarantee of a match. It is a place to describe a real project and meet people who want to help.

## What to post

Start a new discussion in [Collaboration](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/collaboration) using one of these templates:

- [Project proposal](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/templates/PROJECT_PROPOSAL.md) — turn an idea into a scoped proposal.
- [Find a teammate](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/templates/FIND_A_TEAMMATE.md) — recruit a specific role.
- [Collaboration request](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/templates/COLLABORATION_REQUEST.md) — ask for help on an existing project.
- [Code review request](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/templates/CODE_REVIEW_REQUEST.md) — request focused feedback on a change.

## What every post should include

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **The problem** | Helps people decide whether they care |
| **The first milestone** | Keeps the commitment bounded |
| **Skills needed** | Avoids vague "anyone interested" posts |
| **Time commitment** | Respects people's availability |
| **Collaboration model** | Paid, unpaid, voluntary, open source, equity, or hackathon |
| **Ownership and credit** | Prevents disputes later |
| **License** | Clarifies what others can use |
| **Decision rights** | Explains who decides when there is disagreement |
| **Communication channel** | Keeps the work discoverable and safe |

State the collaboration model clearly:

- **Paid:** scope, rate or budget, currency, payment schedule, and relationship.
- **Unpaid / voluntary:** that it is unpaid, the expected time, credit, and license.
- **Open source:** license, contribution process, review expectations, and credit.
- **Equity or revenue share:** written terms, decision rights, and what happens if the project stops.
- **Hackathon team:** event rules, roles, submission ownership, and deadlines.

Do not describe unpaid work as a guaranteed opportunity. Do not promise equity, revenue, or a job without written terms.

## How to respond

- Ask a clarifying question before volunteering.
- Share the relevant work you have done.
- State your available time and preferred communication style.
- Do not send mass direct messages or ask for private contact details immediately.
- If you are not a fit, say so respectfully.
- If you are the builder, respond to every serious offer, even with a short "not right now."

## Safety and moderation

- No fees to join a project or apply for a role.
- No requests for bank details, identity documents, passwords, or API keys.
- No mass unsolicited messages or repeated recruitment posts.
- No harassment when someone declines.
- No misrepresentation of funding, users, revenue, or affiliation.
- No "guaranteed job" or "guaranteed income" offers.
- Disclose paid, unpaid, voluntary, equity, and sponsorship relationships.
- Follow the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [Moderation Policy](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/MODERATION_POLICY.md).

## A good first milestone

A first milestone should be small enough to finish and useful enough to test the collaboration:

- A 30-minute review of the onboarding flow.
- One accessible component with tests.
- A README rewrite for a new contributor.
- A small bug fix with a regression test.
- A design critique with three concrete recommendations.

Do not start with "build the whole product." Start with something that lets both people decide whether to continue.

## What this is not

- Not a job board. Use [Career & Opportunities](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/career-opportunities) for paid roles and jobs.
- Not a guarantee of a teammate, co-founder, or funding.
- Not a place to collect personal data or move people to a private group before trust is established.
- Not a competition to see who can promise the most.
