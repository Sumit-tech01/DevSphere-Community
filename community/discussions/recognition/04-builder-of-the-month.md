# 🏗️ Builder of the Month — Nominate Someone Who Kept Building

Not every meaningful contribution fits a polished project profile. Sometimes the story is a beginner who shipped their first working app, a maintainer who quietly fixed the documentation, or a builder who kept going after a difficult month.

**Builder of the Month** is a monthly community thank-you for meaningful contribution, learning progress, and project development.

It is not an employee-of-the-month contest, a ranking, or a prize. There is no cash award, certificate, job, internship, funding, or investment attached.

## What it recognizes

- A meaningful contribution to a project, resource, or community process.
- Visible learning progress, persistence, or a thoughtful pivot.
- A project that reached a real milestone with honest documentation.
- Consistent helpfulness that made someone else's work possible.
- Work that is easy to miss: tests, accessibility, documentation, moderation, or onboarding.

A small contribution can be enough. The program does not reward volume, follower count, or self-promotion.

## How to nominate

1. Read the [Developer Spotlight handbook](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/DEVELOPER_SPOTLIGHT.md) for the criteria and consent rules.
2. Ask the person for permission. Do not nominate someone without their knowledge.
3. Copy the [Developer Spotlight nomination template](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/templates/DEVELOPER_NOMINATION_TEMPLATE.md).
4. Post a new General Discussion titled `Builder of the Month nomination: <name>`.
5. Include specific evidence and working links.
6. Confirm consent and disclose your relationship to the nominee.
7. Explain what changed because of their work, not only what they shipped.

Self-nominations are welcome. Mark the self-nomination checkbox.

## What happens next

1. A maintainer checks completeness, consent, and category.
2. Reviewers use the same criteria as the Spotlight.
3. The review group considers learning progress and contribution, not only shipped features.
4. A recipient is selected only when the evidence is sufficient. There is no quota.
5. The recipient reviews the recognition draft for factual accuracy.
6. The recognition is published in **Announcements** and indexed in the Spotlight handbook.

A nomination does not guarantee recognition. A month with no well-evidenced nomination may pass without a post. That is a normal outcome, not a failure.

## What the recognition includes

- The chosen name and optional role.
- A specific description of the contribution and why it mattered.
- One or two approved links.
- A short quote only if the person approves the exact wording.
- A note that the recognition is a community thank-you, not a prize or certification.

## Consent and privacy

- Do not post another person's name, project, or story without permission.
- Do not include private contact information, identity documents, customer data, or private messages.
- If the nominee is a minor, do not publish personal details without a parent or guardian's informed consent.
- A recipient can ask for correction or removal at any time.

## What this program is not

- Not a prize, cash award, gift, certificate, job, internship, or funding offer.
- Not a performance review.
- Not a ranking of other builders.
- Not a paid placement.
- Not a promise of future recognition.
