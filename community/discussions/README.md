# DevSphere Discussion Posts

This directory contains twelve ready-to-publish discussion posts for the DevSphere community. Each post includes hidden metadata at the top with its recommended title, category, format, and labels. The hidden comment is not visible when the post is rendered on GitHub.

> **Do not publish all twelve posts at once.** A community should feel lived-in, not filled with scheduled content. Publish the essential orientation posts first, then space the rest over the first two weeks.

## Recommended publishing order

### Before publishing

1. Apply the 12-category structure from [DISCUSSION_SETUP.md](../DISCUSSION_SETUP.md).
2. Move existing discussions #2 and #3 into Introductions and Project Showcase.
3. Create the labels listed in this document, or remove labels that do not exist.
4. Verify every link in the post.
5. Replace any date, challenge number, or status text that will be stale.
6. Confirm the post is not a duplicate of an existing discussion.

### Week one

1. **Announcements:** launch post (01) or refresh existing #1.
2. **Introductions:** welcome/orientation post (02), pinned.
3. **Introductions:** introduce yourself (03), using existing #2.
4. **Project Showcase:** share your projects (04), using existing #3.
5. **AI & Machine Learning:** seed discussion (06).
6. **Web Development:** seed discussion (07).
7. **Career & Opportunities:** seed discussion (10).
8. **Collaboration:** open-source opportunities (11).

### Week two

1. **Collaboration:** find collaborators (05).
2. **Hackathons & Challenges:** weekly challenge progress thread (08).
3. **Hackathons & Challenges:** hackathons and competitions (09).
4. **General Discussion:** first weekly feedback thread (12).

### Ongoing

- Refresh the weekly feedback thread with a new date and a summary of what changed.
- Publish new challenge threads when a challenge opens.
- Keep pinned posts to three or four.
- Do not publish a new announcement for every minor repository change.

## Voice and maintenance

- Keep posts welcoming, specific, and free of hype.
- Do not invent member counts, partnerships, sponsors, or achievements.
- Update dates and links when a post is refreshed.
- Archive outdated challenge threads by closing comments or adding a final status note.
- If a post performs poorly, ask why instead of reposting it repeatedly.
