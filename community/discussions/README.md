# DevSphere Discussion Posts

This directory contains twelve prepared general discussion posts for the DevSphere community. Each post includes hidden metadata at the top with its recommended title, category, format, and labels. The hidden comment is not visible when the post is rendered on GitHub.

The [`recognition/`](recognition/README.md) subdirectory contains five additional drafts for the Developer Spotlight and Community Recognition program. Those drafts are indexed separately and are not counted among the twelve general posts.

> **Do not publish all posts at once.** Three launch discussions are already live and pinned. A community should feel lived-in, not filled with scheduled content. Publish the remaining posts only when there is capacity to respond.

## Recommended publishing order

### Before publishing

1. Confirm the live configuration with [DISCUSSION_SETUP.md](../DISCUSSION_SETUP.md).
2. Do not move or repost discussions #1, #2, or #3. They are already correctly categorized and pinned.
3. Create the labels listed in this document, or remove labels that do not exist.
4. Verify every link in the post.
5. Replace any date, challenge number, or status text that will be stale.
6. Confirm the post is not a duplicate of an existing discussion.
7. Confirm there is capacity to reply to the thread.

### Optional first wave

The three launch posts already cover the essential orientation. Any additional seed post should have a specific purpose.

1. **Announcements:** keep existing #1; refresh only if the launch message needs an update.
2. **Introductions:** keep existing #2. Do not post 02 or 03 as duplicates.
3. **Project Showcase:** keep existing #3.
4. **AI & Machine Learning:** seed discussion (06), if hosted.
5. **Web Development:** seed discussion (07), if hosted.
6. **Career & Opportunities:** seed discussion (10), if hosted.
7. **Collaboration:** open-source opportunities (11), if hosted.

### Week two

1. **Collaboration:** find collaborators (05).
2. **Hackathons & Challenges:** weekly challenge progress thread (08).
3. **Hackathons & Challenges:** hackathons and competitions (09).
4. **General Discussion:** first weekly feedback thread (12).

### Ongoing

- Refresh the weekly feedback thread with a new date and a summary of what changed.
- Publish new challenge threads when a challenge opens.
- Keep pinned posts to three or four.
- Do not publish a new announcement for every minor repository change.

## Voice and maintenance

- Keep posts welcoming, specific, and free of hype.
- Do not invent member counts, partnerships, sponsors, or achievements.
- Update dates and links when a post is refreshed.
- Archive outdated challenge threads by closing comments or adding a final status note.
- If a post performs poorly, ask why instead of reposting it repeatedly.
