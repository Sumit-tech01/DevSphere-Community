# Welcome to DevSphere — Start Here

Welcome. You are in the right place.

DevSphere is a global developer community for people who want to learn, build, and grow together. Whether you are writing your first line of code, preparing for your first internship, maintaining an open-source project, or launching a company, there is a place for you here.

This post is the quickest way to understand the community and make your first move.

## What DevSphere is

- A **free** community hosted on GitHub.
- A place for **technical discussion**, project feedback, collaboration, and career growth.
- A community built around **GitHub Discussions**, not a private chat app.
- A space where **beginners are welcome** and questions are treated as a normal part of learning.

## What DevSphere is not

- It is not a paid course, bootcamp, or job guarantee.
- It is not a place for spam, affiliate links, or get-rich-quick schemes.
- It is not a replacement for official documentation.
- It is not a finished product. It is a community being built in public, and you can help shape it.

## Your first five steps

1. **Introduce yourself** in the Introductions category. A short hello is enough.
2. **Read the [Community Guidelines](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md)** so we share the same expectations.
3. **Follow the categories** that match your interests, such as Programming, AI & Machine Learning, or Web Development.
4. **Ask one question or answer one question.** Participation is how a community becomes useful.
5. **Share a project** in Project Showcase when you are ready.

## How to be a great community member

- **Be respectful.** Critique ideas, not people.
- **Be specific.** Include context, code, errors, and what you tried.
- **Be generous.** Share what you know and credit what you use.
- **Be patient.** Volunteers answer across time zones.
- **Protect privacy.** Never post credentials or someone else's personal data.
- **Stay curious.** "I don't know yet" is a valid starting point.
