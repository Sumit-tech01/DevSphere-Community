# DevSphere Builders of the Month

**Builders of the Month** is DevSphere's monthly community thank-you for meaningful contribution, learning progress, and project development. It exists for work that may not fit a single Developer Spotlight profile: the documentation fix, the test suite, the patient review, the beginner who shipped a first working version, or the maintainer who kept a project alive during a difficult month.

> **Status:** No builder has been recognized yet. DevSphere does not invent recipients, achievements, quotes, or statistics. A month with no consented, well-evidenced nomination is a normal month.
>
> **Cadence:** monthly review cycle. There is no quota and no requirement to publish a recognition post every month.

## What it recognizes

- A meaningful contribution to a project, resource, or community process.
- Visible learning progress, persistence, or a thoughtful change in direction.
- A project that reached a real milestone with honest documentation.
- Consistent helpfulness that made someone else's work possible.
- Work that is easy to miss: tests, accessibility, documentation, moderation, or onboarding.

The same evidence and consent rules as the [Developer Spotlight](DEVELOPER_SPOTLIGHT.md) apply. Stars, followers, commit counts, seniority, and posting volume are not selection criteria.

## How the monthly cycle works

| Step | When | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nomination call | First Monday of the month, 09:00 UTC, when a host is available | [General Discussion](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/general-discussion) |
| Nomination review | During the month, when nominations exist | Private maintainer review |
| Consent and draft review | Before any publication | Private conversation with the nominee |
| Recognition post | Late in the month or the first week of the next month, when ready | [Announcements](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/discussions/categories/announcements) |
| Archive update | After publication | This page |

The schedule is a target, not a promise. A month with no nomination, no reviewer capacity, or no consent may pass without a recognition post.

## How to nominate

1. Read the [recognition criteria](DEVELOPER_SPOTLIGHT.md#recognition-criteria) and the [nomination template](templates/DEVELOPER_NOMINATION_TEMPLATE.md).
2. Ask the nominee for permission if you are nominating another person.
3. Use the title `Builder of the Month nomination: <name or project>`.
4. Provide specific evidence and working links.
5. Post in **General Discussion** and disclose any conflict of interest.
6. Do not nominate someone without their permission.

Self-nominations are welcome. A nomination is not selection, and a previous nomination does not guarantee future recognition.

## Selection and consent

- Reviewers use the same evidence rubric as the Developer Spotlight.
- Reviewers consider learning progress and contribution, not only shipped features.
- Reviewers do not rank people by stars, followers, commit count, or posting volume.
- A person can be recognized more than once, but not automatically or repeatedly without new evidence.
- The review group records a short reason privately and shares it with the nominee if asked.
- No name, project, quote, or image is published until the nominee has reviewed and approved the final draft.
- A nominee can decline at any time, including after accepting an invitation.
- Reviewers with a conflict of interest recuse themselves.

## Recognition post format

A Builder of the Month post includes:

- The chosen name and optional role.
- What the person contributed and why it mattered.
- One or two specific examples, with approved links.
- A short quote only if the person approves the exact wording.
- A note that the recognition is a community thank-you, not a prize or certification.
- Links to the person's approved project or contribution.

The post should be specific enough that a reader can understand what happened without knowing the person. It should not compare the recipient to another member.

## What it is not

- Not a prize, cash award, gift, certificate, job, internship, or funding offer.
- Not a performance review.
- Not a ranking of other builders.
- Not a paid placement.
- Not a promise of future recognition.
- Not a claim that one contribution is more valuable than another.

## Corrections and removal

A recognized person can ask for a correction or removal at any time through the private reporting route in the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Do not repost removed content or confront the person publicly. See the [Developer Spotlight privacy and removal rules](DEVELOPER_SPOTLIGHT.md#privacy-corrections-and-removal) for the full process.
