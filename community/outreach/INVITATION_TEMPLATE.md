# Developer Spotlight Invitation Template

Use this template for a **private** invitation. Replace the bracketed prompts, remove anything that does not apply, and do not send it automatically.

> **Do not publish this as a discussion post.** Do not include private contact details, assumptions about the person's identity, or claims that have not been verified. An invitation is not a selection announcement.

## Subject line

`Invitation: DevSphere Developer Spotlight — [project or contribution]`

## Body

Hello [preferred name],

I am [your name], [role] at DevSphere. We are reaching out because [specific public project or contribution] was shared in the DevSphere community, and we would like to invite you to be considered for a **Developer Spotlight**.

### What we noticed

[One or two specific, verifiable sentences about the project or contribution. Link to the public repository or page. Do not use inflated language or unverified metrics.]

### What the Spotlight is

The Developer Spotlight is a consented, evidence-based profile of a developer and a project they choose to share. It is a community thank-you, not a ranking, certification, prize, job offer, funding offer, or endorsement of every claim in the project.

If you are interested, the process is:

1. A short written interview using a template we provide.
2. A review of the draft for factual accuracy.
3. Your explicit approval of the name, links, project description, and any quote.
4. Publication in the DevSphere **Announcements** category, only if you approve.

The interview usually takes 30–45 minutes. Most questions are optional. You can skip location, social links, photos, or any personal story you do not want to publish. No live call or recording is required.

### What we need from you

- A yes, no, or "tell me more" — any of these is welcome.
- If yes, the public name, links, and project details you want considered.
- If you are not interested, just say so. We will close the invitation and will not mention you publicly.

There is no deadline and no pressure. A decline has no effect on your membership, moderation, or future opportunities in DevSphere.

### What we will not do

- Publish your profile without explicit permission.
- Publish private contact information or unverified claims.
- Promise money, prizes, certificates, jobs, internships, funding, or investment.
- Use your project for paid placement or affiliate promotion.
- Pressure you to answer optional questions.

You can read the full process here: [Developer Spotlight handbook](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/DEVELOPER_SPOTLIGHT.md).

If you would like to participate, reply with a good time to send the interview template. If you have questions, ask them first; there is no obligation to decide now.

Thank you for building in public.

[Your name]
[Role], DevSphere
[Public contact link approved by DevSphere]
