# 🌟 The First DevSphere Developer Spotlight Invitation

> **Draft discussion post.** Intended for **General Discussion**. Publish manually only. Do not publish automatically. No candidate is named or selected by this post.

This is the first invitation round for the DevSphere Developer Spotlight.

It is an invitation to participate, not an announcement that someone has won. **No one has been selected yet.** There is no shortlist, no deadline, and no requirement to publish a profile every week.

## Who can be nominated?

Anyone who participates in DevSphere or has contributed publicly in a way the community can review:

- Beginners, students, and self-taught developers.
- Independent builders and open-source contributors.
- Designers, technical writers, testers, and accessibility advocates.
- AI and machine-learning practitioners.
- Community helpers who answer questions, review work, or welcome new members.

You do not need a large following, a senior title, a degree, or a famous repository. A small, well-documented project or a pattern of patient help can be strong evidence.

## How to take part

1. Read the [Developer Spotlight handbook](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/DEVELOPER_SPOTLIGHT.md).
2. If you are nominating another person, ask them first and confirm the name, links, and details they are comfortable making public.
3. Copy the [Developer nomination template](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/templates/DEVELOPER_NOMINATION_TEMPLATE.md).
4. Post a new discussion in General Discussion with the title `Developer Spotlight nomination: <name or project>`.
5. Complete the consent section and disclose any conflict of interest.

Self-nominations are welcome.

## What happens next

1. A maintainer checks completeness, category, and consent.
2. Reviewers check the evidence against the published criteria.
3. Anyone with a conflict of interest does not decide that nomination.
4. Shortlisted nominees receive a private invitation.
5. If they accept, they complete the interview template and approve the final draft.
6. Approved profiles are published in **Announcements**.

A nomination is not selection. A first round without a published profile is possible, and that is not a failure.

## Privacy and consent

- Do not post another person's name, project, or story without asking them.
- Do not include private contact information, identity documents, customer data, or private messages.
- If you want to suggest someone without their knowledge, contact a maintainer privately instead of posting a public nomination.
- If the nominee is a minor, do not publish personal details without a parent or guardian's informed consent.
- A featured person can request correction or removal at any time.
