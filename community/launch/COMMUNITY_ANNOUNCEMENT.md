# 🌟 The DevSphere Recognition Campaign Starts Here

> **Draft community announcement.** Intended for the **Announcements** category. Publish manually only. Do not publish automatically.

DevSphere is opening its first community recognition campaign: a consent-first way to notice the people who build useful things, help others learn, review work carefully, and keep projects moving.

**No one is featured yet.** There is no shortlist, no winner, and no quota. The first profile will be published only after a nomination, an evidence review, a private invitation, and the person's explicit approval.

## Four ways to participate

| Initiative | What it does | Where it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Developer Spotlight** | A written profile of a developer and a project they choose to share | Nominations in General Discussion; published profiles in Announcements |
| **Project Review Friday** | Specific, constructive feedback on a community project | Project Showcase |
| **Builder of the Month** | Monthly recognition for contribution, learning progress, and project development | Nominations in General Discussion; recognition in Announcements |
| **Community Collaboration** | Help finding collaborators, reviewers, and teammates | Collaboration |

These programs use the existing 12 discussions categories. No new category is needed.

## How to take part

- **Nominate yourself or someone else.** If you nominate another person, ask them first and confirm the name, links, and details they are comfortable making public.
- **Bring a project to Project Review Friday.** Share one project and one specific question. Use the [Project Review template](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/templates/PROJECT_REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md).
- **Nominate a builder for the monthly recognition.** Use the [Developer nomination template](https://github.com/Sumit-tech01/DevSphere-Community/blob/main/community/templates/DEVELOPER_NOMINATION_TEMPLATE.md) and choose **Builder of the Month** in the title.
- **Look for a collaborator.** Describe a real project and the role you need in Collaboration.

## What recognition is not

It is not a ranking, a popularity contest, a certification, a prize, a job offer, a funding offer, or a paid placement. Stars, followers, commit counts, seniority, and posting volume are not selection criteria. A small documentation fix or a patient answer to a beginner can be strong evidence.
