# DevSphere Recognition Campaign Launch Kit

Prepared launch assets for the first DevSphere Developer Spotlight and Community Recognition campaign. The kit connects the public announcement, the founder's introduction, the first invitation round, the first Project Review Friday session, and the LinkedIn launch post.

> **Status:** Prepared, not published. No recognition campaign discussion has been posted, no LinkedIn message has been sent, and no candidate has been contacted. Every asset is manual-publish only.

## Assets

| Asset | Channel | Purpose | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Community announcement](COMMUNITY_ANNOUNCEMENT.md) | GitHub **Announcements** | Introduce the full recognition campaign | Draft |
| [Founder introduction](FOUNDER_INTRODUCTION.md) | GitHub **General Discussion** or LinkedIn | Explain why recognition is consent-first | Draft |
| [First Spotlight invitation](FIRST_SPOTLIGHT_INVITATION.md) | GitHub **General Discussion** | Open the first invitation round without naming anyone | Draft |
| [First Project Review Friday](FIRST_PROJECT_REVIEW_FRIDAY.md) | GitHub **Project Showcase** | Open the first review session | Draft |
| [LinkedIn announcement](LINKEDIN_ANNOUNCEMENT.md) | LinkedIn | Announce the campaign to a wider audience | Draft |

The recurring discussion drafts live in [`community/discussions/recognition/`](../discussions/recognition/README.md). This kit contains campaign-specific launch copy, not replacements for the recurring threads.

## Suggested publishing order

1. Confirm the repository links, templates, and CI checks are live.
2. Publish the **community announcement** in Announcements.
3. Publish the **founder introduction** in General Discussion.
4. Publish the **first Spotlight invitation** in General Discussion.
5. Publish the **first Project Review Friday** thread on a Friday when a reviewer is available.
6. Publish the **LinkedIn announcement** after the community links are live.
7. Send personalized invitations privately. Do not post a candidate's name before consent.

Space the posts over several days. Publishing all five at once is not recommended.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent participants, projects, achievements, quotes, or statistics.
- Do not announce a spotlight recipient before the person has approved the final draft.
- Do not use member counts, follower counts, or growth claims as proof of success.
- Do not tag people who have not agreed to be tagged.
- Do not promise jobs, internships, funding, prizes, certificates, or viral reach.
- Keep every asset free of private contact information and unverified claims.
- Publish manually. Do not schedule or automate a post.
